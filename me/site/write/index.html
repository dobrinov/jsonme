Write your me.json

From your LinkedIn PDF to a {me} CV in 10 minutes.

The AI you already use writes the file. It reads your old CV, then asks you for the proof and numbers it can't know. Paste the result here to check it and see your CV before you publish. Nothing you paste leaves your browser.

  1. 1

    Give your AI the prompt

    Open a new chat in Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Paste the prompt and attach your LinkedIn PDF (on LinkedIn: your profile → More → Save to PDF) or an old CV. Answer its questions; say "just write it" whenever you've had enough.

    Read it first ↗

    If your AI can open links, this one line is enough: Read https://dobrinov.github.io/jsonme/prompt.md and help me write my me.json.

  2. 2

    Paste what it gives you

    Paste the whole answer, code fences and all. An old JsonMe file works too; it is converted for you.

  3. 3

    Optional: add how you use LLMs

    An agent that can read your past prompts finds your patterns: how you plan, build and review with AI. Claude Code keeps your prompts on your machine in ~/.claude/projects. Open it in any folder, paste the prompt, and tell it to read those files. Your prompts stay on your machine, and you choose what goes on the CV.

    Read it first ↗

    Or one line for Claude Code: Read https://dobrinov.github.io/jsonme/patterns-prompt.md and apply it to my prompts in ~/.claude/projects.

  4. 4

    Check the preview, then save the file

    This is your CV as it will look when published. Not happy with a part? Tell your AI what to change and paste the new version above.

    Your CV shows here once you paste a file.
